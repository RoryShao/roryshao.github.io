About Me

Renrong Shao is an Assistant Professor and a Postdoctoral Fellow working under the supervision of Prof. Lulu Zhang (Changjiang Scholar) at the Naval Medical University. He received his Ph.D. degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology, East China Normal University, in June 2024, co-advised by Prof. Jun Wang (Young Thousand Talents Scholar) and Prof. Wei Zhang. His main research interests include Model Compression, Transfer Learning, AI Generated Content (AIGC), Agentic Science. Currently, as the first author, he has published more than 10 papers in top-tier international journals and conferences, including IEEE TIP, IEEE TCSVT, ICCV, and PR, among others. He also serves as a reviewer and/or Program Committee (PC) member for several respected journals and conferences, such as IEEE TIP, IEEE TKDE, IEEE TCSVT, ACM MM, CVPR, and AAAI. He has led or participated in multiple medical research projects and was selected for the Navy Medical Science and Technology Young Talent Cultivation Program in 2026.

Research

My research interests include:

  • Model Compression: Knowledge Distillation (KD), Model Quantification.

  • Transfer Learning: Data-free Knowledge Distillation(DFKD), Source-free Domain Adaptation (SFDA).

  • Generated Content (AIGC): Large Language Model (LLM), Stable Diffusion Model (SD).

  • Agentic Science: Agentic Medical Task Analysis, Agentic Basic Medical Research.
